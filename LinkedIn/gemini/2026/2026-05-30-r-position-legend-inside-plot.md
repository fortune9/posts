🚀 Tired of legends taking up half your plot space in ggplot2?

In R, maximizing the data-ink ratio often means moving the legend inside the plot area. It’s easier than you think!

Key steps:
✅ Use `legend.position = c(x, y)` for relative positioning.
✅ Anchor with `legend.justification`.
✅ Clean it up with `legend.background = element_blank()`.
