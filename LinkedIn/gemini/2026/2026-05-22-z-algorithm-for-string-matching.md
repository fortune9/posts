When searching for patterns in massive datasets, the naive $O(n \times m)$ approach just doesn't cut it. That's where the **Z-algorithm** comes in—a linear-time powerhouse that simplifies string matching.

Key takeaways:

✅ **Linear Efficiency**: Achieves $O(n + m)$ time complexity.

✅ **The "Z-box"**: Remembers previously matched prefixes to skip redundant comparisons.

✅ **Mirroring Strategy**: Reuses computed values for maximum performance.
