Today at church, a pastor shared something that stayed with me.

His wife passed away last year. He missed her so deeply that he had prayed for God to take him too. As he spoke about his hope of seeing her again in heaven, he reflected on living a life that pleases God.

His words made me ask myself: What kind of person do I want to be? 💭

If today were my last day on earth:

• What would I do immediately?
• Which of my worries would still matter?
• How would I treat the people around me?

These questions put my current challenges into perspective, including the search for my next role. The uncertainty is real, and some days are difficult. But this is a season of my life. I believe I can grow through it. I believe you can grow through it, too 🌱

They also remind me to appreciate what I have: my health, my family, my friends, and the people who care about me. ❤️

I am not guaranteed that everything I have today will still be here tomorrow. I want to cherish these gifts now—to make the call, spend the time, express my gratitude, and be more patient and present.

Thinking about our limited time on earth might sound sad. For me, it brings hope. I trust that God has a plan for my life, even when I cannot see what comes next. 🕊️

Today, I have people to love, opportunities to grow, and a chance to live according to my faith.

That makes every day a great day. 🙏☀️
