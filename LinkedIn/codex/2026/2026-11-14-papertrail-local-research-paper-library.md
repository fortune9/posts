Ever downloaded too many scientific papers, then struggled to find them again when you needed them? 📚

I’ve collected plenty of PDFs across different folders. Over time, I started losing track of what I had downloaded—and which paper contained the finding I remembered.

So I built Papertrail, a local research paper library, using the openworkerai/openworker-harness-builder agent skill. 🛠️

Here’s what it does:

📂 Indexes scientific PDFs from any local folder, including subfolders, and stores extracted text in a SQLite database. Your PDFs stay in their original locations.

🔎 Searches your collection by title, author, abstract, or full text, with matching passages and links to the relevant PDF pages.

💬 Lets you ask questions about your search results, using Codex with ChatGPT sign-in or a model provider configured with your own API key. Answers draw on retrieved excerpts and include source-page links.

Indexing and search work offline, without an AI account. The AI assistant is optional.

My goal is simple: spend less time hunting through downloaded papers and more time reading, connecting ideas, and doing research.

If your Downloads folder has become an unofficial literature database, give Papertrail a try. I’d love to hear what would make it useful for your workflow.
