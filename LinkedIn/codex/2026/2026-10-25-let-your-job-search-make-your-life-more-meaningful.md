# Let Your Job Search Make Your Life More Meaningful

If you’re searching for your next job, I’m one of you. It’s hard, and you’re not alone.

I’m trying to approach this season from a different angle: acknowledging what I’ve lost while paying attention to what I can gain.

For me, that means:

🪞 **Reflecting on my value.**
When I was working, my work formed a large part of how I understood my worth. Without that role, I’ve had to ask: Who am I beyond my job? What do I contribute to the people around me?

🌱 **Learning for the years ahead.**
Job searching has introduced me to skills I’m excited to develop—not only for my next position, but for the next five to ten years. Interviews have become opportunities to learn, too, even when they don’t lead to offers.

🤝 **Rebuilding connections.**
I’m reconnecting with friends and former colleagues I haven’t spoken with for a while. Those conversations remind me how much life holds beyond work. The relationships are important part of my life.

🧭 **Thinking about what makes life meaningful.**
What matters most to me? Who do I want to become? What kind of life do I want my work to support?

I know that needing a job to pay the bills makes these reflections difficult. The financial pressure is real. I’m continuing to work hard to find my next role while making room for these questions.

A clearer sense of purpose can give us strength through the search—and guide how we contribute when the next opportunity comes.

🙏 My faith reminds me that God has a plan for me. My part is to follow His guidance, keep learning, and take the next step.

What has this season of job searching taught you about yourself?
